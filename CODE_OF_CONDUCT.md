# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful, assume
good intent, discuss technical decisions with evidence, and protect private device
or user data. Maintainers may remove abusive, discriminatory, threatening, or
privacy-violating contributions and may restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through repository contact
channels. Maintainers will review reports fairly and protect reporter confidentiality.
